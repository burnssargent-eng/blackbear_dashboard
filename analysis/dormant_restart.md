# Consistency gate vs dormant-gap restart

Generated by `analysis/backtest_dormant_restart.py`. **Production** = the builder before this change (the seasonal formula for strict seasonal-open customers, the 50/50 for everyone else). **Gate** = Sarge's idea: when the last n gaps span at most W days and their interval rates have a coefficient of variation at most c, the pooled rate of those gaps. **Restart** = a gap longer than D days inside either production window with 3+ pickups since: the pooled rate of the post-gap pickups (at most 6 gaps) — the builder's `dormant_restart`. Every rate is total gallons ÷ total days, never a mean of interval rates. Choose = 2021–23, confirm = 2024–26; closers left out. Intervals resample whole customers.

**Decision (2026-10-09): the restart at 180 days (= `STALE_DAYS`) is adopted; the consistency gate is rejected.** Every gate variant is worse on the confirm years; where it fires it over-projects, because a run of agreeing pickups is partly luck and the next one reverts — production is already nearly unbiased on frequent, steady customers. The restart fires on few pickups but they are among the worst misses, and it improves both periods; 120 days does better on choose but its confirm interval crosses zero, and 180 is a threshold the builder already uses.
