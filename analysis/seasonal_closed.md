# Seasonal-closed detector

Generated by `analysis/backtest_seasonal_closed.py`. A customer is a **detected closer** for test year Y when the k complete years before Y (2020 skipped) average to a shape with a cyclic block of at least M months below T of an average month. **The shape keeps zero months as zero**: `cf.factor_from_years` sets a month with no production in any year to 1.0, which hides a closure. Guards, not tuned: at least 300 gal/yr and a median gap of at most 60 days (longer gaps leave artificial empty months behind the 60-day spread). **Truth** = the same block test on year Y itself. Choose = 2022–23, confirm = 2024–25.
