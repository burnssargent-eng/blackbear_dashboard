## Caveats

- **Pickups not scored:** 18 (a model could not be formed — usually too little history for the two-year level rate).
- **Survivorship.** The sample requires a pickup in every year 2021–2026.
- **The bootstrap treats pickups as independent,** so true intervals are wider.
- **Closers are unsolved.** A closed month is a real zero; a multiplier cannot fix a rate × gap model across a closure.
