## Caveats

- **The bootstrap treats pickups as independent.** Consecutive pickups share most of their training window, so their errors are correlated and the true intervals are somewhat wider than shown. A difference that only just clears zero should be read as a lean, not a result.
- **Hand-picked sample.** Eleven customers chosen because they look steady. A best case, not an estimate for the customer base.
