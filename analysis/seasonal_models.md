- **steady** — the 11 hand-picked customers in `backtest_steady_rate.py`

- **closes seasonally** — the same, but the quietest month produces under 10% of an average month

## Mechanics

- **steady:** blend used both halves on 856 of 963 pickups (year ago only 107, recent only 0). Ratio fell back to last 6 on 1; median ratio 1.02, middle 90% 0.79–1.30, beyond 0.5–2× on 8.
- **seasonal:** blend used both halves on 152 of 327 pickups (year ago only 175, recent only 0). Ratio fell back to last 6 on 0; median ratio 1.03, middle 90% 0.59–1.75, beyond 0.5–2× on 15.
- **closes seasonally:** blend used both halves on 82 of 128 pickups (year ago only 46, recent only 0). Ratio fell back to last 6 on 4; median ratio 1.05, middle 90% 0.36–3.48, beyond 0.5–2× on 39.
- **Pickups not scored** (a model could not form a rate): Quechee Gorge Snack Bar 2.

## Caveats

- **The seasonal group was chosen with the same years it is scored on.** The score uses 2023–25 and so do the tests. That is fine for asking whether seasonal models help seasonal customers; a production classifier would score from history before each prediction.
- **The bootstrap treats pickups as independent.** Consecutive pickups share most of their windows, so true intervals are somewhat wider.
- **Small groups.** 11 steady, 6 seasonal, 3 closes seasonally customers. Check the by-customer table before trusting a pooled figure.
