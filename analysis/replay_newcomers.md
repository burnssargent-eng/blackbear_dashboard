# Phase 3: replaying newcomers from their first pickup

Generated by `analysis/backtest_replay_newcomers.py`. Every customer whose first pickup is on or after 2021-01-01 is replayed pickup by pickup, using only what was known before each pickup. The **first pickup is a starting point only** — its gallons never enter a rate. Shared-barrel members are modelled on their own; customers who later left are included. **Choose** = pickups dated 2021–23 (band widths and the will-call rule are set here); **confirm** = 2024–26.

Error measures: **WAPE** on gallons at pickup ("how full is it now"); **bias** (negative = under-projects, the costly direction); **days** = median absolute error in the date the barrel reaches 75% of capacity ("when will it hit 75%"), where the capacity is known.

354 newcomers; 280 have at least one scorable pickup after the first; 280 are active today. 138 pickups that came more than 180 days after the previous one are left out, as production holds such customers out of the ranking (a closer's off-season, a sporadic bulk account).
